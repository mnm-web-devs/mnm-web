Roadmap

From zero to competition level

Get curious

Read BrainFacts.org and explore its 3D brain. Learn the main parts of the brain and what they do.

Weeks 1–4

Build the base

Learn neuron biology: action potentials, synapses, neurotransmitters. Khan Academy biology helps here.

Months 1–3

Go deeper

Work through a university-level course such as Medical Neuroscience (Duke, Coursera) with an MNM study group.

Months 3–8

Compete

Enter your local or national Brain Bee and aim for the International Brain Bee.

Season

Do research

Read papers, join an MNM research project and present what you learned.
