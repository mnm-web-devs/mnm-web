Flagship initiative

Les Enfants du Monde

A proposed eight-week cohort in the DRC connecting mathematics, neuroscience, medicine, AI literacy and practical business skills, so young people can find local problems, design solutions and test small ventures.

Designed with local educators, not just for them. Materials localised in French and Lingala. Delivery, scope, cohort size, partners and budget depend on local consultation and safeguarding readiness.

Our commitments
