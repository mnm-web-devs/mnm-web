About MNM

Built by students who couldn't pick just one science.

MNM is a YIMO-affiliated STEM initiative for students who love medicine, neuroscience and mathematics, and want to see how they connect.

The biggest questions in medicine and the brain are now answered with mathematics. We think students should learn them together, not in separate boxes.

What we believe

Our values

01

Curiosity first

No one needs to be an expert to join. Asking good questions matters more than knowing answers.

02

Learn by doing

We learn through olympiads, research and real projects, not only by reading.

03

Students lead

MNM is run by students. Every team has student leads who make real decisions.

04

Open to everyone

Joining is free, and members come from different schools and countries.
