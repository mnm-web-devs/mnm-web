FAQ

Questions

Who can join?

Students who are curious about medicine, neuroscience or mathematics. You don't need to be an expert in any of them.

Does it cost anything?

No. MNM is a student-run initiative and joining is free.

When are the meetings?

We meet online. Times are announced in the Discord so members in different time zones can plan ahead.

How much time do I need?

As much as you want to give. Members join what they like; staff agree on their workload with their team lead.

Can I be in more than one track?

Yes. MNM is about how the three sciences connect, so many members follow more than one.
