Privacy

Your data, in plain words

MNM is run by students, and many of our members are under 18. We collect as little as possible and never sell or share it.

What we collect

  • When you just visit: nothing personal. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts and scripts are served from our own site, not from Google or other third parties.
  • Visit statistics: we may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) that counts page views without identifying you.
  • When you fill in a form (workshop registration, partnership or volunteer form, account sign-up): your name, email and the answers you type in.

Why we use it

Only to run MNM: to send you workshop links, reply to your message, and manage your account and challenge submissions. We never sell your data and never use it for ads.

Under 16?

Please ask a parent or guardian before signing up or filling in a form. If you are a parent and want your child's data removed, email us and we'll delete it.

Who can see it

Only the MNM leads who need it for the task (for example, the team running a workshop). Data is stored with the services that power our forms and accounts.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data. Just email medineuromatics@gmail.com.
