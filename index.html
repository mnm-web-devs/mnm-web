What we do

Seven ways to grow with MNM

  1. LearnSessions, study groups and curricula built by students, for students.
  2. CompetePrepare for olympiads and competitions together.
  3. ResearchRead papers, ask questions and work on real research projects.
  4. CreateBuild websites, tools, content and designs that share science.
  5. CollaborateWork with members from different schools and countries.
  6. LeadRun a team, a program or an event and learn by leading it.
  7. ImpactUse what we learn to help people outside our community.
